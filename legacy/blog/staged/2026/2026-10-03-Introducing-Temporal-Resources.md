Temporal resources are a new, experimental feature in Ash: resources that remember every state their records have ever been in, so you can read and write them as of any moment, past, present or future, with a single option.

The article is interactive, so it lives on a page of its own: [read it here](https://ash-hq.org/blog/introducing-temporal-resources).
